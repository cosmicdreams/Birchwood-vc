# Questions to answer before we build

These go with the draft diagrams in `workflows.html`. Write answers straight under each question. "Don't know yet" is a useful answer too, because it tells us what to research.

Questions marked ★ change the shape of the build the most, so answer those first.

## The business

1. ★ How many properties and units does Birchwood have today, and how many in two years?
2. ★ Does Birchwood own all its buildings, or does it also manage buildings for other owners? If it manages for others, do those owners need reports or their own sign-in?
3. Which cities or states are the properties in? Rent, notice, late-fee and eviction rules depend on this.
4. Who works at Birchwood today, and what does each person do? List names or roles: leasing, accounting, property managers, supers, management.
5. ★ What software runs the business now? For example AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, a spreadsheet, QuickBooks, or a CRM such as HubSpot. What do people like about it, and what do they work around?
6. ★ Are we replacing those tools, or building a website that connects to them?

## Keeping the tenant list

7. ★ Where is the tenant list kept today, and who is allowed to change it?
8. What does a complete tenant record contain? For example everyone on the lease, emergency contacts, pets, vehicles, guarantors.
9. How are applicants screened today (credit, background, income), and by what service?
10. Are leases signed on paper or electronically, and with what tool?
11. How long must records of past tenants be kept?

## Rent collection

12. ★ How do tenants pay today: online, check, money order, cash, ACH? What share uses each?
13. What is the current on-time payment rate, and what rate would count as success?
14. What are the grace period and late fee? Do they differ by property or by lease?
15. Who calls a tenant who is late, and when? Is there a script or a set of steps they follow?
16. Are payment plans offered? Who approves them?
17. At what point does a late account go to formal notice or legal action, and who decides?
18. Which bank or payment processor would online payments go through?

## Bringing in a new property

19. ★ Walk us through the last property you brought on. What happened first, what took longest, and what fell through the cracks?
20. Is there a written ramp-up checklist today? Could you share it?
21. Who signs off that a property is ready to go live?
22. Your brief has a blank second item under management. What else does management do on the site? Some guesses: owner reporting, budgets, approving vendor invoices, staff performance.

## Tenants and the portal

23. ★ What do tenants contact the office about most often today?
24. Which documents should a tenant be able to see: their lease, house rules, inspection reports, payment history?
25. Which dates matter most to tenants? How far ahead should each reminder go out, and by email, text or both?
26. How does renewal work today? Who decides the new rent, and when is the tenant told?
27. Does every building have its own super? How do tenants reach them today?
28. What counts as an emergency, and who answers the phone after hours?
29. Should the super see requests in the same system? Do they need a phone-friendly view?
30. What languages do tenants need the portal in?

## Supers and vendors

31. Who are the regular vendors (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, cleaning, landscaping), and how are they chosen?
32. Who can send a vendor out, and is there a spending limit that needs approval above it?
33. Do we need to track vendor insurance certificates, licenses and contracts?
34. How are vendor invoices paid today, and should that live in this system?

## Public site, leads and analytics

35. ★ Where do new renters come from today: listing sites such as Zillow and Apartments.com, signage, referrals, social media?
36. What happens to an inquiry today, who follows up, and how quickly?
37. Are tours given in person by staff, self-guided, or virtual?
38. Should the site show live availability and pricing, or only invite an inquiry?
39. ★ Which numbers would you want to see every week? Some candidates: visitors, inquiries, tours, applications, leases signed, cost per lease.
40. Which of the three brand directions should the public site use? Or is that still open?

## Trust and safety

41. Which records are sensitive (Social Security numbers, bank details, screening reports), and who should be able to see them?
42. How do we keep the public site and screening compliant with fair-housing rules?
43. Do any outside parties need access, such as owners, accountants or lawyers?
