01The color story

Warm ink. Soft edges.

Oxblood brings the character; buttercream leaves room to breathe. Apricot and dusty blue feel collected, not coordinated. Charcoal keeps everyday reading easy.

Oxblood
#632F3BIdentity · headlines · primary actions

Charcoal
#2D2929Body copy · practical details

Working rule: dark type on the light colors; buttercream type on oxblood or charcoal. Accent colors are surfaces, not small text on cream.

03The signature

An original, in good company.

A softly expressive wordmark, paired with a spare B monogram. Its upright strokes and small cross-street detail echo the rhythm of a city block — no rooftops required.

The name leads; the monogram signs off. Keep generous clear space and use a single ink when production calls for simplicity. Wordmark shown as live type for this prototype; final artwork would be custom-refined and outlined.
